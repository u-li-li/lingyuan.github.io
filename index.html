01 / About

At the beginning of the research journey.

My doctoral work is taking shape through reading, experimentation, and conversations with people who see the problem differently.

This site is a living record of that process. As the work develops, I’ll use it to share research directions, useful notes, and the ideas that survive careful testing.

For now, the focus is simple: build strong foundations, stay curious, and make the work understandable to others.

02 / Approach

How I want to do research.

  1. 01

    Questions first

    Start with the real problem. Define it clearly before reaching for a familiar method.

  2. 02

    Evidence over instinct

    Make assumptions visible, test them carefully, and let the evidence change the direction.

  3. 03

    Progress in the open

    Share ideas early enough for feedback to improve them, and explain the work without hiding behind jargon.
